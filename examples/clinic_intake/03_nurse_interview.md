# Interview notes: triage nurse (30 min)

Today she copies the clipboard form into the EHR by hand, about 4 minutes per patient, and makes typos when it is busy.

- Nurses must be able to flag urgent symptoms (chest pain, trouble breathing) so the patient jumps the queue.
- She wants allergies and current medications shown at the top of the patient summary screen.
- Nurses should record vitals (blood pressure, temperature, weight) in the same place as the intake.
- Doctors need to see the nurse's notes and vitals before they enter the room.

She is worried about elderly patients who are not comfortable with tablets. Front desk staff should be able to fill in the form for a patient.

Unclear whether vitals belong in this app or stay in the EHR.
