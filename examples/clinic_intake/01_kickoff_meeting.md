# Kickoff meeting: Riverside Community Clinic intake app

Attendees: clinic manager, two nurses, front desk lead, IT volunteer

## Goal
The goal is to replace the paper sign-in sheet and clipboard intake forms with a simple digital intake app, so patients wait less and nurses stop re-typing forms into the EHR.

## What we heard
- Patients need to check in on a tablet in the waiting room without creating an account.
- The intake form must work in English and Spanish.
- Front desk staff need to see the live queue of who has checked in and how long they have waited.
- Nurses want to review the intake answers before the visit starts, on one screen per patient.
- Intake form fields: name, date of birth, phone, preferred language, reason for visit, insurance provider, allergies.
- The clinic manager wants a weekly dashboard showing average wait time and no-show rate.
- We should send an SMS reminder the day before each appointment.
- It would be nice to have a self check-in kiosk mode that locks the tablet to the app.

## Concerns
- Everything with patient data has to be HIPAA compliant and encrypted.
- Wi-Fi in the waiting room drops often, so check-in must keep working offline and sync later.
- Not sure yet whether we push intake answers into the EHR or just export a PDF.
